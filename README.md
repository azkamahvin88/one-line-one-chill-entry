A make.fun entry for **One Line, One Chill**: one line of horror — 94 characters, a single sentence — that has to frighten a reader in one breath. The program writes exactly that line to `/output/entry.txt`.

> The dentist pulled my tooth, held it up, and asked why I had someone else's tooth in my mouth.

A routine dental visit turns wrong in the last clause: the tooth the dentist just pulled is not the narrator's. The scare is not the tooth — it is what the tooth implies, that something was put inside the narrator's body, or that the narrator is not who he thinks he is. No gore, no ghost, no mirror, no doll, no basement; the dread is earned by the final phrase, "in my mouth", which lands the blow inside the reader's own body.

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```
